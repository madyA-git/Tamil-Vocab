The Three Key Formulas of Heritage Tanglish

1. The English Verb Formula

To use an English verb, take the bare action stem and append paṇṇu (do). This converts it instantly into a grammatically active Tamil verb.
Example: click + paṇṇu = click-paṇṇu (to click/photograph)

2. The Noun "U" Buffer

English nouns ending in a hard consonant receive a soft terminal -u vowel, aligning the phonetics naturally into Tamil sentence structures.
Example: Cold + -u = Cold-u (The winter cold)

3. Agglutinative Case Suffixes

Case markings (possessive, dative, locative) are appended directly to the end of nouns, whether they are English or Tamil.
Example: Sofa + -la = Sofa-la (On the sofa)
